About the Thank You Teacher Raffle

More than a raffle — a movement to build Ghana's first self-sustaining cancer care & research centre, led by the Cancer Foundation of the Ghana National Association of Teachers' Fund.

Who we are

A promise from the teachers of Ghana

The Thank You Teacher Raffle is an initiative of the Cancer Foundation of the Ghana National Association of Teachers' (GNAT) Fund. It was created to honour the educators who have shaped every profession in our nation — and to turn that gratitude into something that heals.

Cancer touches families in every community in Ghana, yet access to world-class diagnosis, treatment and research remains out of reach for far too many. Too often, care means travelling abroad, or going without. We believe Ghana can, and should, care for its own.

So we set an ambitious goal: to raise GHC 100 million every year through this raffle, and to use it to build and sustain a cancer care and research centre that funds its own future — a lasting national asset that will serve Ghanaians for generations.

Every ticket you buy — for just GHc 20 — is a brick in that foundation. And along the way, we're giving back to our supporters with life-changing prizes.

🎯

Our Mission

To raise GHC 100 million annually through the Thank You Teacher Raffle, financing the establishment of Ghana's premier, self-sustaining cancer care and research centre through the Cancer Foundation of the GNAT Fund.

🌅

Our Vision

A Ghana where every person facing cancer can access world-class care and research at home — funded by a centre built to last, and championed by the teachers who built our nation.
