Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket?

Each Thank You Teacher Raffle ticket costs GHc 20. You can buy as many as you like — more tickets mean more chances to win.

Who can take part?

The raffle is open to everyone aged 18 years and above. You'll be asked to confirm your age when you buy.

What can I win?

First Prize: 5 two-bedroom houses · Second Prize: 5 electric cars · Third Prize: 5 motorcycles · Second Level Draw: 10 cheques valued at GHS 15,000 each — 25 winners in total.

Where does my money go?

Proceeds support the Cancer Foundation of the Ghana National Association of Teachers' Fund, raising GHC 100 million annually to build and sustain Ghana's premier cancer care & research centre.

How will I know if I've won?

Winners are drawn after ticket sales close and contacted directly using the details provided at purchase. Results are also published officially.
