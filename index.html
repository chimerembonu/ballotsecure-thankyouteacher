About

Everyone has a teacher story.

We leave their classrooms. We grow up. We move on.

But some of what they gave us stays. The confidence to try. The discipline to keep going. The belief that we could become more than we imagined.

Thank You, Teacher is an annual national fundraising initiative by the Ghana National Association of Teachers (GNAT), created to recognise the teachers whose influence reaches far beyond the classroom.

Each year, this initiative brings Ghanaians together to celebrate teachers while raising funds to support a cause that matters to the nation.

Impact

A thank you that goes further.

Thank You, Teacher is an annual fundraising initiative, with its area of impact potentially changing from year to year.

For this year's campaign, proceeds will support the GNAT Cancer Foundation and its vision for stronger cancer care and research capacity in Ghana.

Questions? We've got answers.

What is Thank You, Teacher?

Thank You, Teacher is an annual national fundraising initiative by the Ghana National Association of Teachers (GNAT), created to recognise the contribution of teachers while raising funds for causes that matter to Ghana.

What is this year's initiative supporting?

For 2026, proceeds will support the GNAT Cancer Foundation and its vision for stronger cancer care and research capacity in Ghana.

Will the initiative support the same cause every year?

Not necessarily. Thank You, Teacher is an annual initiative, and the area of impact may change from year to year.
